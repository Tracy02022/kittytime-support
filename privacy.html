Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 28, 2025

KittyTime (“we”, “our”, or “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data when you use our mobile application.

1. Data We Collect

  • Contact Information: Your email address is collected during registration for account management purposes.
  • User Content: You may enter logs including body fat, weight, mood, personal notes, and future letters or audio capsules. These entries are stored securely and associated with your account.
  • Identifiers: We assign each user a unique ID to store their data and personalize their experience.
  • Analytics Data: We use Firebase Analytics to gather anonymized usage data to improve app performance and user experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To store and sync your personal logs and data in the cloud
  • To display personalized statistics and visualizations
  • To manage your future messages and mood tracking features
  • To enhance app usability and performance through usage insights

3. Data Storage and Security

All data is securely stored using Firebase services with encryption and authenticated access. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

4. Your Rights and Choices

You have full control over your data. You may request data deletion or account removal by contacting us. You can stop using the app at any time.

5. Children’s Privacy

KittyTime is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect or store personal data from children under this age.
